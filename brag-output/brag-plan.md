# Brag Plan: QR Volde

## What is this app?

QR Volde is a free, client-side 3D web experience where a giant QR code unfolds into a living Kathmandu chowk — and then lets you make a scannable QR of your own.

## The angle

The QR is not a utility graphic. It is architecture.

Most QR-code marketing shows a flat square on a white background. QR Volde turns the code into a place: a pagoda, a tea stall, a stone spout, a small dog, prayer flags, and the Himalayas. The launch film should make that transformation feel like a place you want to step into, then land on the practical promise: you can make your own code in seconds.

The tone is quiet wonder, not hype. Let the world do the talking.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

A huge, tactile QR wall fills the right side of the frame. The line **"Every square is a door."** arrives on the left. Before the viewer can finish reading it, the wall starts to move.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The QR wall breaks into the chowk as the camera pulls up and back: pagoda, shop row, tea stall, dhara, stupa, trees, people, bus.
- The detail is specific and Nepalese: tiered Newari roofs, brick and timber, prayer flags, a stone spout, a small dog, Himalayan peaks.
- **QR Studio** proves it is a real tool, not just a render: a real screenshot of the studio, with a high-contrast QR preview and a **Download PNG** action.
- The final frame gives the viewer a clear next step: **Create your QR.**

## Outro / punchline

The world settles into a wide, calm composition. The wordmark and line land:

**QR Volde**
**Every square is a door.**
**harmless-beep.github.io/qr-volde**
**Create your QR.**

The last shot should feel like a place, not a product page.

## User flow worth showing

Entry → scan the QR world → make your own code.

1. Land on the giant QR and scroll.
2. Watch the QR become a Kathmandu chowk.
3. Open QR Studio, see the scannable preview, and take the **Create your QR** action.

The video shows the first two beats as one continuous camera move, then shows the real Studio UI as the third.

## Tone

- **Preset:** polished
- **Creative direction:** quiet premium product film with a touch of wonder
- **Interpretation:** restrained typography, long holds, soft transitions, no hard-sell language. Motion comes from the world, not from decorative loops.

## Audio direction

- **Role:** warm, restrained bed with a small lift at the end.
- **Music:** "Happy Beats / Business Moves, Vol. 10" by Sascha Ende (CC BY 4.0), first 20 seconds.
- **Music treatment:** start at 0.0, gentle fade-in over 0.4s, sit under the copy, fade out from 19.1–20.0.
- **Music cue guidance:** track tempo ≈110 BPM. Strong cue at **18.55s** for the final CTA/wordmark landing. Beat grid around **4.6s**, **10.6s**, and **15.8s** for scene cuts.
- **Audio-reactive treatment:** subtle; a barely perceptible warmth/glow on the final city plate. No waveform graphics.
- **SFX posture:** sparse and soft. Two short whooshes at the major scene seams and one quiet click as the Studio card arrives.
- **Audio-coupled moments:** the QR-to-city reveal, the Studio card entrance, and the final wordmark.
- **Restraint rule:** no big trailer hits, no whooshes over the dialogue-free text, no music that competes with the final URL.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Signal — 4.6s

The 3D QR wall fills the right two-thirds of the frame. A warm cream field on the left holds the copy:

- Eyebrow: **01 — THE SIGNAL**
- Headline: **Every square is a door.**
- Body: **A QR code that becomes a living Kathmandu chowk.**
- Small line: **SCROLL TO UNFOLD**

The QR wall is real app footage, not a recreation. At 0.5s the camera begins its scroll; by the end of the scene the wall has started to break apart.

Sequential/interaction: The headline arrives in two lines; the body follows; the scroll line fades in last. No cursor.

Audio intent: establish warmth and curiosity. The music is present but not loud.

Audio-coupled idea: a soft whoosh under the first camera move at 4.0s.

Music: warm bed.

Transition mood: continuous camera move → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The Chowk — 6.0s

The camera continues its single take. The QR modules have become the plaza. The city assembles around it.

- Eyebrow: **02 — THE CHOWK**
- Headline: **Kathmandu, in code.**
- Body: **Every square becomes a place.**
- Four tags arrive one by one: **PAGODA**, **TEA STALL**, **DHARA**, **ONE SMALL DOG**
- Small line: **KEEP SCROLLING**

The footage shows the actual pagoda, shop row, tea stall, dhara, stupa, trees, people, bus, and mountains. No generic city footage.

Sequential/interaction: Four small tags appear in sequence, each held long enough to read. The camera keeps moving behind them.

Audio intent: the world opens up; the music gains a little air.

Audio-coupled idea: tiny soft ticks for the tags, if they do not clutter the mix.

Music: warm bed, slightly more present.

Transition mood: soft cut from live footage to the final city plate → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — QR Studio — 5.2s

The final chowk plate sits behind a warm cream gradient. A real QR Studio screenshot slides in as a card.

- Eyebrow: **03 — QR STUDIO**
- Headline: **Make your own.**
- Body: **Any link. Any story. A scannable code in seconds.**
- CTA pill: **Create your QR.**
- Small line: **FREE · CLIENT-SIDE · NO BACKEND**

The screenshot shows the actual studio, its QR preview, the input field, and the **Download PNG** action. The card is treated as a real product artifact, not a fake mockup.

Sequential/interaction: The Studio card arrives first; the copy follows; the CTA is last. A small cursor-like dot moves toward the card but does not fake a click.

Audio intent: a small, satisfying shift from “world” to “tool.”

Audio-coupled idea: one quiet click as the Studio card lands.

Music: same bed, slightly brighter.

Transition mood: card and copy exit cleanly; the city plate remains → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The Door — 4.2s

The final city plate is wide and still, with a slow, almost imperceptible settle. The wordmark lands on the left.

- **QR Volde**
- **Every square is a door.**
- **harmless-beep.github.io/qr-volde**
- CTA pill: **Create your QR.**
- Small line: **FREE · NO BACKEND · MADE WITH THREE.JS**
- Music credit: **Music: Sascha Ende / ende.app (CC BY 4.0)**

The final frame should be postable as a still.

Sequential/interaction: Wordmark, tagline, URL, and CTA land in that order. The CTA lands near the 18.55s strong cue and holds.

Audio intent: resolve, not explode. Let the music fade under the URL.

Audio-coupled idea: a soft sparkle or low chime on the wordmark, then nothing but the bed.

Music: warm bed, fade out 19.1–20.0.

Transition mood: fade to cream/black end.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, optimistic, restrained.

**Audio summary:** A quiet, upbeat bed carries the single camera move; small whooshes mark the two seams; the Studio click makes the tool feel real; the final CTA lands on a strong cue and fades out.
